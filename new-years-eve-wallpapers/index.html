About this collection

These 60 New Year's Eve wallpapers are picked for the night itself, December 31: fireworks over midnight cities and harbours, the ball drop, rooftops above the party, and tables set with champagne. If the happy New Year page is the greeting, this page is the countdown.

Eve scenes are the boldest in the collection — night skies, gold light and confetti — and they are framed for a phone lock screen, with the burst or the skyline high and the clock readable beneath it. Every image is portrait-shaped (1152 × 2048) for iPhone and Android screens.

All 60 are free in full quality with no watermark. Set yours before the evening starts — fireworks for the countdown, sparklers for the walk home — and switch to a first-sunrise scene from the New Year 2027 collection on January 1.

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
