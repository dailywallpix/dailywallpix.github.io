About this collection

This collection brings together 104 city wallpapers made for phone screens — a tour of the world's skylines without leaving your lock screen: neon Tokyo in the rain, the New York skyline at blue hour, Paris in golden light, Dubai's marina sparkling at night, and London streets shining wet. The pack keeps travelling — Hong Kong towers, a Santorini hillside, Venice canals, old Kyoto lanes, and a run of night harbours and skylines from Manila Bay to Beijing and Guangzhou — so there is a city here for every mood, not just the famous four.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so towers and streets run the full height of the frame instead of being cropped into a strip. Rain and night scenes dominate the pack, because that is when cities photograph best — reflections double the light, and the darker skies keep your clock and notifications easy to read. The golden-hour scenes — Paris, Santorini, the foggy hills above the bay — are the warm daytime picks.

All 104 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so neon, headlight trails and skyline detail stay sharp on any modern phone. Set the city you dream about, the city you came from, or the one you are saving to visit — and change it whenever wanderlust moves on.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the skyline stays in frame.
