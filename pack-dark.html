About this collection

This collection brings together 24 dark wallpapers made for phone screens — the true-black end of the library: a neon wave and a neon wolf drawn on pure black, a black rose under rim light, black sand under stars, a midnight forest in fog, and a black cat whose amber eyes do all the shining. Around them sit the moody scenes — night drives, a lighthouse in a black sea, a castle under storm clouds, a train crossing black mountains — every one built portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen.

Looking for a black wallpaper 4K? Most of this collection sits on pure black or near-black backgrounds, which is exactly what OLED and AMOLED screens are made for: black pixels switch off completely, saving battery, and the clock and notifications stay razor sharp against the dark. The black marble, black ink and black lotus pieces are the purest black wallpapers in the set; the neon pieces add a single glowing subject and nothing else.

All 24 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so the blacks stay pure and the glow stays sharp on any modern phone. Start with the neon wave for pure black, the night drive for mood, or the blood-moon dragon for drama — and swap whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the glowing subject sits below the clock.
