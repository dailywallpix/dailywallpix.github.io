About this collection

This collection brings together 62 sunset wallpapers made for phone screens — the sky showing off: a lake on fire at sunset, pink clouds stacked at dusk, a golden field under the last light, and storm light breaking before dark. The pack runs the whole evening — foggy hills at first light's opposite, twilight deepening to first stars, palms against a beach sunset, sunbeams through cloud, a couple silhouetted on the sand, and a cactus standing in a desert sky.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the gradient of the sky runs the full height of the frame — deep at the top, glowing at the horizon — instead of being cropped to a band. That gradient is a gift for lock screens: the darker upper sky keeps the clock crisp while the colour does its work below. The pink-cloud and twilight pieces are the soft picks; the storm glow and lake fire are the dramatic ones.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so cloud texture and horizon light stay smooth on any modern phone — no banding in the gradients. Set the sunset you ended today with, or the one you are saving for — golden hour lasts all day on a lock screen.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the horizon sits low in the frame.
