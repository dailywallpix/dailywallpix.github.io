About this collection

This page holds 60 hand-picked wallpapers for iPhone — chosen for tall OLED screens and the way the iPhone lock screen actually looks. Dark scenes come first, because a dark background makes the big clock and notifications pop, and goes easy on battery; then soft aesthetic scenes that sit quietly behind widgets without fighting them. Every wallpaper is 1152 × 2048, so it fills your iPhone screen edge to edge with no cropping and no blurry stretch.

A good iPhone wallpaper has to work around the clock. The picks here keep their main subject low in the frame, so the time, date and notifications stay readable at a glance, and the depth-effect wallpapers in the set let the subject rise in front of the clock on supported iPhones for that layered look. Dark pieces suit the always-on display too — the dimmed lock screen stays readable without glowing in a dark room — while the lighter aesthetic picks are the ones to choose when you want the home screen to feel calm and uncluttered.

All 60 are free in full quality with no watermark, and they work on every iPhone that takes a 1152 × 2048 image — from the latest Pro models back through the older tall-screen iPhones. Pick a dark one for the lock screen and an aesthetic one for the home screen, or use the same image on both; changing it takes ten seconds whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves to your Photos in full quality.
  2. Open it in Photos, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper.
  3. Choose Lock Screen, Home Screen, or both — drag to position the subject below the clock, then tap Set.
