About this collection

This collection brings together 104 flower wallpapers made for phone screens — tulip rows at sunrise, lavender at golden hour, sunflowers towering into a summer sky, dark roses, a wild mountain meadow, soft peonies, morning daisies, a rose garden arch, cherry blossom skies, rain-wet hydrangeas, orchids in close-up, lotus at dawn, wisteria curtains, coral dahlias, purple irises, and lilies on a still pond. From classic garden roses to wildflower fields, every bloom here is photographed-style, bright, and made to be looked at daily.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the bloom, the bouquet, or the field stays in frame instead of getting cropped away. The soft pastel scenes — peonies, cherry blossom, lavender — suit a calm, gentle home screen, while the bold ones — red roses, sunflowers, marigolds — make a lock screen full of colour. They suit garden lovers, spring birthdays, and anyone who wants their phone to feel fresh the moment they pick it up, in any season, since these gardens never wilt.

All 104 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so petals, dew drops, and morning light stay sharp on any modern phone. Pick the flower that feels like yours — a tulip for spring, a sunflower for summer, a rose for someone special — and change it whenever the season or the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
