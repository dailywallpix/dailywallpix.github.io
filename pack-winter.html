About this collection

This collection brings together 62 winter wallpapers made for phone screens — the cold months at their most beautiful: a frozen lake under pale light, pines heavy with snow, an ice cave glowing blue, a frozen waterfall stopped mid-fall, and a red fox crossing a white field. The human side of the season is here too — a ski village at dusk, a cabin window glowing against the snow, a snowman standing in an empty field, a reindeer herd, and skaters on a frozen pond.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so drifts, forests and frozen water fill the frame instead of being cropped away. Winter scenes are naturally bright at the top and darker at the edges, which keeps the clock readable on the pale pieces, while the cabin and village scenes run warm and dark for night use. Snow scenes also flatter OLED screens — pure whites stay crisp and the blue shadow detail holds.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so ice crystals, snowfall and firelight stay sharp on any modern phone. Start with the frozen lake for calm, the ice cave for drama, or the cabin window for warmth — and let the season change on your screen before it changes outside.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
