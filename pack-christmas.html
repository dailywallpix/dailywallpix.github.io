About this collection

This collection brings together 104 Christmas wallpapers made for phone screens — glowing Christmas trees in cozy rooms, snowy streets lined with lit windows, reindeer under northern lights, cabins built for cocoa, and the smaller details people love at this time of year: ornaments in close-up, stockings by the fireplace, gingerbread houses, Christmas markets, candy-cane paths, and Santa's sleigh crossing a winter sky.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the tree, the street, or the reindeer stays in frame instead of getting cropped away. The collection mixes warm and calm: bright, golden scenes for people who want their phone to feel festive the moment they pick it up, and quieter snowy scenes — a first snowfall, a candlelit window, a cabin in the woods — for a calmer December look. They suit family phones, gifts for someone who loves Christmas, and anyone who changes their wallpaper with the season.

All 104 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so the lights and snow stay sharp on any modern phone. Pick the scene that matches your December — a golden tree for the living-room feeling, a snowy street for a quiet evening, or a reindeer for a bit of magic — and change it again whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
