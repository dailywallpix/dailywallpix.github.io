About this collection

This is the Christmas wallpaper aesthetic in one page: 60 soft, glowing scenes picked from the DailyWallPix Christmas collection for their light rather than their subject. Candle rows in a snowy chapel, ornaments dissolving into bokeh, an angel topper glowing gold, hot cocoa by a window full of fairy lights.

Aesthetic Christmas wallpapers suit a calmer December — warm, low-contrast scenes that sit quietly behind your icons instead of shouting over them. The candlelit and night scenes keep the top of the frame dark enough for a readable clock, while the white tree and snow globe pieces keep a bright, clean feel for the home screen.

All 60 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark. Start with the candlelit chapel for pure calm, the bokeh tree for glow, or the snow globe for the storybook feeling — and see the full Christmas collection for the louder side of the season.

How to set one as your wallpaper
