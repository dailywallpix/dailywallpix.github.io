About this collection

These 30 dark wallpapers for iPhone are the best of our dark collection, picked for how they sit on an iPhone lock screen. Deep blacks come first — true black backgrounds that make the big clock and notifications pop and go easy on battery — then night skies, city lights and glowing AMOLED art, each one framed so the main subject sits low and leaves the clock readable.

A dark wallpaper earns its place by doing two jobs at once: it looks dramatic, and it keeps the screen readable. Every image here is genuinely dark (low-brightness scenes, verified from the image itself), not a bright photo with a dark filter, so the clock stays legible even on the always-on display and the screen never glares in a dark room.

All 30 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, sized to fill tall iPhone screens edge to edge with no cropping. Pick a true black for the lock screen and a night-sky scene for the home screen, or use the same image on both — changing it takes ten seconds whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves to your Photos in full quality.
  2. Open it in Photos, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper.
  3. Choose Lock Screen, Home Screen, or both — drag to position the subject below the clock, then tap Set.
