About this collection

This collection brings together 1,310 dark wallpapers for phone screens — the darkest corner of the library: true-black AMOLED art where a single glowing subject does all the talking — a neon wolf, a black rose, neon mountains, a gold wave on black, a neon jellyfish and a neon dragon. Around them sit the moody night scenes the dark look is famous for — night drives, black sand under stars, midnight forests in fog, a lighthouse in a black sea and low-light city corners — every one built portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen.

Dark is the easiest look to live with on a phone. On an OLED or AMOLED screen a true-black background switches those pixels off completely, which saves a little battery every single day, and at night the same dark scenes go easy on your eyes, with the clock and notifications standing out cleanly instead of fighting a bright photo. Dark tones also make app icons and widgets pop on the home screen, and they keep a busy lock screen looking calm.

All 1,310 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so the blacks stay pure and the glow stays sharp on any modern phone. Start with a neon piece if you want the lock screen to glow, a true-black scene if battery matters most, or a moody night shot if you just love the look — and swap whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the glowing subject sits below the clock.
