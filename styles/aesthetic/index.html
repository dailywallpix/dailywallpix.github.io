About this collection

This collection brings together more than 140 aesthetic wallpapers for phone screens — the softest corner of the library: a pastel sky at dawn, beige walls with soft leaf shadows, cherry blossom drifting over a quiet street, silk gradients in peach and pink, a coffee flatlay on a slow morning, linen in morning light, pampas grass in a calm corner, pastel clouds, a bookstore corner, a Paris cafe, lavender fields at a soft sunset, and a pastel galaxy for nights when the sky should feel like a dream. Further in sit the quiet details — a cream knit sweater flatlay, fog layers over mountains, cream curtains in sunlight, macarons, a butterfly macro, a daisy field, a pink shore, a typewriter, a pier, a pastel bedroom, a soft pink sky, and a peach sunrise over mountains.

Aesthetic is the look people choose when the phone itself should feel calm. The pale, low-contrast scenes suit light-mode home screens especially well — whites, beiges and pastels keep icons readable and the whole phone feeling tidy — while the dusk and blue-hour pieces make a gentle lock screen for evenings. The soft light never fights the clock or notifications, and the scenes suit anyone who picks a wallpaper the way they pick a room — for how it makes them feel: students, journal keepers, cafe lovers, and everyone who wants a beautiful screen without the noise.

All of them are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so the soft light and pastel colours stay smooth on any modern phone. Pick the mood that fits today — a pastel sky for a fresh start, a cozy cafe for a slow afternoon, a lavender field for a quiet evening — and change it whenever the season or the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene sits below the clock.
