About this collection

These 60 merry Christmas wallpapers collect the scenes people picture when they say the words: a tree lit gold in a cozy room, a village under snow with every window glowing, a Christmas market at dusk, bells and holly, and a sleigh crossing the village green. They are drawn from the heart of the DailyWallPix Christmas collection.

Nothing here is minimal or ironic — this is the warm, traditional side of the season, made for the lock screen you will look at all December. The lit windows and candlelight scenes keep a soft glow behind the clock, and every image is portrait-shaped for phone screens, so the tree or the village fills the frame.

All 60 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark. Pick one for the whole season or change with the weeks of December — tree first, market in the middle, candles for Christmas Eve — and find even more in the full Christmas collection.
