About this collection

This collection brings together 62 rain wallpapers made for phone screens — for everyone who loves the sound of rain: drops beading on city glass, a cabin window looking onto a wet forest, one red umbrella on a grey street, and a leaf sparkling with rain. The moods run from shelter to storm — a window seat with tea, a forest path in the downpour, neon streets shining wet, a storm watched from behind glass, puddles doubling the city lights, and rain bokeh softening everything behind the pane.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the glass, the drops and the scene behind them fill the frame instead of being cropped away. Rain scenes are naturally gentle on a lock screen: the blurred, watery backgrounds keep icons and the clock easy to read, and the grey-blue tones stay calm at night. The neon street and puddle pieces are the colourful picks; the cabin window and window seat are the cozy ones.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so drop detail and wet-street reflections stay sharp on any modern phone. Set the rainy window on the days you are staying in — or on the days you wish you could.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the rainy scene stays in frame.
