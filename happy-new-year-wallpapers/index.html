About this collection

These 60 happy New Year wallpapers are the celebration half of our New Year 2027 collection — the loud, bright, raise-a-glass scenes. Fireworks come first, over the city, the beach, the lake and the bridge; then the gold: glitter, balloons, confetti and the toast itself.

New Year is the biggest wallpaper week of the year, and these are made for countdown week — bold scenes that fill a lock screen and survive a hundred glances a day. Every image is portrait-shaped (1152 × 2048), so the fireworks burst above the clock instead of being cropped away.

All 60 are free in full quality with no watermark. Set a fireworks scene for New Year's Eve, keep the gold for the first week of January, and browse the full New Year 2027 collection — including the quiet sunrise scenes — when you are ready for the fresh start.
