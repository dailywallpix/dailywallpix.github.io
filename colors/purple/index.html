About this collection

This collection brings together 54 purple wallpapers for phone screens — every image here earned its place because purple is its dominant colour, measured pixel by pixel across the whole library. Violet nebulas and spiral galaxies sit beside purple auroras, lavender fields and wisteria tunnels, neon-violet streets and deep purple gradients, so the collection covers the whole purple look rather than one corner of it.

Purple is the colour of the in-between hours — dusk skies, aurora light, neon after rain — which is why so many of these scenes happen at the edge of night. It is rich enough to feel premium yet, at its deep end, calm behind a clock and icons; on OLED screens the violet night skies and smoke pieces glow against near-black, while the lavender and wisteria pieces keep a softer daylight feel.

All 54 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so star fields, petal detail and neon reflections stay crisp on any modern phone. Start with a nebula for deep space, an aurora for the night sky, or lavender for a calmer screen — and swap whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the violet subject sits below the clock.
