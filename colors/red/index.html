About this collection

This collection brings together 77 red wallpapers for phone screens — every image here earned its place because red is its dominant colour, measured pixel by pixel across the whole library. Canyon rock and desert stone sit beside crimson sunsets, red maple forests, scarlet flowers and neon-red city light, so the collection covers the whole red look rather than one corner of it.

Red is the loudest colour on any home screen — energy, confidence and warmth in one glance, and a lock screen that is impossible to ignore. The deeper crimson and canyon tones stay easy on the eyes at night, while the bright scarlet pieces give daytime screens real punch behind dark icons. Autumn does half the work here: maples, leaves and low sun turn whole landscapes red for free.

All 77 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so rock texture, petal detail and sunset gradients stay crisp on any modern phone. Start with a canyon for drama, a maple forest for autumn, or a crimson sunset for golden hour — and swap whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the red subject sits below the clock.
