About this collection

This collection brings together 51 pink wallpapers for phone screens — every image here earned its place because pink is its dominant colour, measured pixel by pixel across the whole library. The result runs from cherry blossom tunnels and pink peonies through magenta sunset skies and pink salt lakes to neon-pink city streets and glossy pink gradients, so the collection covers the whole pink look rather than one corner of it.

Pink is the friendliest colour a lock screen can wear. At its soft end — blossom, rosy skies, pink-sand shores — it keeps a home screen calm and pretty behind icons and widgets; at its saturated end — magenta ink clouds, neon alleys, cotton-candy clouds — it turns the phone into a small piece of pop art. White clock text stays readable on pink's deeper shades, and its lighter tones keep daytime screens bright without glare.

All 51 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so petal detail, neon glow and gradient blends stay crisp on any modern phone. Start with blossom for spring days, a pink sunset for golden hour, or a neon street after dark — and swap whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the pink subject sits below the clock.
