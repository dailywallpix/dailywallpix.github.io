About this collection

This collection brings together 322 black wallpapers for phone screens — every image here earned its place because black is its dominant colour, measured pixel by pixel across the whole library. The result runs from pure true-black art, where one glowing subject does all the talking, through deep charcoal night scenes, to soft black-and-gold and black-and-neon pieces. Neon animals, glowing waves, night drives, black sand, midnight forests and low-light city corners all sit side by side, so the collection covers the whole black look rather than one corner of it.

Black is the easiest colour to live with on a phone. On an OLED or AMOLED screen a true-black background switches those pixels off completely, which saves a little battery every single day — and at night the same dark scenes go easy on your eyes, with the clock and notifications standing out cleanly instead of fighting a bright photo. Dark tones also make app icons and widgets pop on the home screen, and they keep a busy lock screen looking calm.

All 322 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so the blacks stay deep and the glow stays sharp on any modern phone. Start with a true-black piece if battery matters most, a neon scene if you want the lock screen to glow, or a moody night shot if you just love the look — and swap whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the glowing subject sits below the clock.
