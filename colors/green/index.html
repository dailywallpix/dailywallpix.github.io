About this collection

This collection brings together more than 150 green wallpapers for phone screens — every image here earned its place because green is its dominant colour, measured pixel by pixel across the whole library. Bamboo leads the way — bamboo rain, a bamboo path in the morning, bamboo mist and a bamboo grove — beside the gardens: buttercups in a green field, white narcissus in spring, a peacock showing its feathers, bluebells carpeting a forest floor and a wildflower meadow below the mountains. Jungle scenes sit alongside them — a red panda in bamboo, a jaguar watching from the leaves, a tiger staring through the jungle, a canopy sloth and a parrot among tropical leaves — so the collection covers the whole green look rather than one corner of it.

Green is the freshest colour a lock screen can wear — the colour of spring mornings, rain on leaves and deep forest shade. It sits quietly behind the clock and notifications, so the time stays readable at a glance, and it keeps a busy home screen feeling calm. The bright meadow and garden pieces suit daytime screens, while the misty redwood forest, jungle rain and emerald gorge give the lock screen a quieter, moodier feel after dark.

All of them are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so leaf detail, fur and feather texture and rain drops stay crisp on any modern phone. Start with bamboo for calm, a garden meadow for spring, or a jungle scene for the wild side — and swap whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the green subject sits below the clock.
