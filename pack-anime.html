About this collection

This collection brings together 52 anime wallpapers made for phone screens — a girl under cherry blossoms, a quiet train ride in the rain, a castle floating in the clouds, the coziest room you have ever seen, a samurai under the moon, a mecha at sunset, a shrine maiden in the forest, a rooftop under the stars, a lofi study corner, a cyberpunk alley, a beach day with friends, a snow-covered station, and a dragon in flight. Every piece is original art made for this site, so the look stays consistent from one wallpaper to the next.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the character, the train, or the sky castle stays in frame instead of getting cropped away. The soft, dreamy scenes — blossoms, a window seat, a night market, a lighthouse — suit a calm home screen, while the bold ones — neon rain, a cyber samurai, a dragon over the cliffs — make a lock screen full of energy. They suit anime fans of every kind, from quiet Ghibli-style moods to bright city nights.

All 52 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so the colours and line work stay sharp on any modern phone. Pick the world you would step into if you could — a blossom tunnel, a night train, a floating castle — and change it whenever a new favourite scene comes along.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
