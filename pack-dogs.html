About this collection

This collection brings together 52 dog wallpapers made for phone screens — a German shepherd in the mist, a Labrador running through the surf, a corgi's famous grin, a husky ruling the snow, a French bulldog tilting its head, a pug's wrinkled face, a golden puppy, a beagle's long ears, and a pile of puppies doing nothing but cuddling. From working shepherds and mountain Bernese to tiny chihuahuas and gentle great Danes, every dog here looks ready to jump off the screen and into your day.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the dog's face stays in frame instead of getting cropped away. The collection suits every mood: a calm, wise old dog for a quiet home screen, a mid-air agility leap or full-sprint field run for a phone that should feel alive, and beach dogs, snow dogs, and Christmas dogs for anyone who changes their wallpaper with the season. They work just as well as a gift of a sort — set one on a dog lover's phone and watch their face.

All 52 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so fur, eyes, and muddy paws stay sharp on any modern phone. Pick the dog that looks most like yours — or the one you wish was yours — and change it whenever a new favourite comes along.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
