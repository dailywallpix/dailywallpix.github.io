About this collection

This collection brings together 62 gaming wallpapers made for phone screens — a neon gaming setup glowing in a dark room, a cyberpunk warrior under city rain, a racing cockpit in a light tunnel, a dragon over a glowing kingdom, a triple-screen racing rig, a forest knight with a glowing sword, an esports arena on a big night, a mecha in its hangar, a rooftop ninja under the moon, a retro arcade room, a VR headset dissolving into particles, and a loot chest spilling golden light. From pixel-art sunsets and cozy farming valleys to boss doors, sky fortresses, and floating islands, the mix covers the games people actually play and the rooms they play them in.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the setup, the warrior, or the dragon stays in frame instead of getting cropped away. The neon and night scenes suit gaming phones especially well — RGB glow, dark rooms, and light trails look striking on OLED screens and sit beautifully on dark-mode home screens. Pick a loud neon one for match nights, or a quiet pixel sunset for everyday use.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark. Save your favourite now — a neon setup for the desk, a dragon for the lock screen, or a cozy valley for the days between sessions.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
