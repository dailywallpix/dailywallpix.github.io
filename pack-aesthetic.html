About this collection

This collection brings together 62 aesthetic wallpapers made for phone screens — a pastel sky at dawn, beige walls with soft leaf shadows, cherry blossom drifting over a quiet street, silk gradients in peach and pink, a coffee flatlay on a slow morning, linen in morning light, pampas grass in a calm corner, pastel clouds, a bookstore corner, a Paris cafe, lavender fields at a soft sunset, and a pastel galaxy for nights when the sky should feel like a dream. From cozy flatlays and cream knit sweaters to fog layers over mountains and cream curtains in sunlight, every scene is soft, calm, and made to be looked at a hundred times a day without ever feeling loud.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the soft gradient, the cafe table, or the pastel sky stays in frame instead of getting cropped away. The pale, low-contrast scenes suit light-mode home screens especially well — whites, beiges, and pastels keep icons readable and the whole phone feeling tidy — while the dusk and blue-hour scenes make a gentle lock screen for evenings. They suit anyone who wants their phone to feel calm: students, journal keepers, cafe lovers, and everyone who picks a wallpaper the way they pick a room — for how it makes them feel.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so the soft light and pastel colours stay smooth on any modern phone. Pick the mood that fits today — a pastel sky for a fresh start, a cozy cafe for a slow afternoon, a lavender field for a quiet evening — and change it whenever the season or the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
