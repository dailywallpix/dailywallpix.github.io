About this collection

This collection brings together 62 autumn wallpapers made for phone screens — the whole season in one pack: a maple tunnel in full colour, a mirror-still lake cabin, a golden forest road, a harvest field at last light, and a squirrel gathering acorns. Cozy scenes sit alongside the wild ones — a pumpkin porch, a lantern path through fallen leaves, a campfire ringed with leaves, and a café window steamed against the cold — while the misty hills, foggy oaks and rain-soaked leaves carry the quieter, moodier side of fall.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the colour fills the frame instead of being cropped away. Warm scenes — the orchard, the covered bridge, the deer meadow at dusk — suit the weeks when the days shorten and you want the phone itself to feel warmer; the fog and rain pieces run darker, which keeps the clock readable and suits night use. Autumn colour is at its best in the weeks around peak foliage, but a golden forest road earns its place on the screen all year round.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so leaf detail, cabin light and mist stay sharp on any modern phone. Start with the maple tunnel if you want the season at full strength, or the misty hills for something calmer — and swap as the weather outside turns.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
