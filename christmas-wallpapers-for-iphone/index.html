About this collection

This page brings together 60 Christmas wallpapers for iPhone, drawn from across the full Christmas collection: the golden tree, snowy streets of lit windows, reindeer under northern lights, Christmas markets and cabins made for cocoa. They were picked for how they sit on an iPhone lock screen — main subject low in the frame, room for the clock at the top.

Setting one takes ten seconds: tap Download, open the image in Photos, tap Share and then Use as Wallpaper. The dark scenes — the night street, the reindeer, the cabin window — suit the always-on display and keep notifications readable, while the bright tree and market scenes are the festive daytime picks.

All 60 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, and the same images fit Android phones too — they are simply presented here the way iPhone owners search for them. For the whole season in one place, see the full Christmas collection.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
