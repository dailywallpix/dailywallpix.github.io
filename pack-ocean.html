About this collection

This collection brings together 62 ocean wallpapers made for phone screens — salt air for your lock screen: a turquoise beach seen from above, a wave lit gold by the sunset, a lighthouse standing at dusk, and a sea turtle gliding over a reef. The pack covers the sea in all its tempers — storm waves breaking, a coral garden in full colour, a hammock strung between palms, a dolphin mid-jump, and a seashell in macro detail on wet sand.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the shoreline and the horizon sit where they belong instead of being cropped away. Water scenes are kind to lock screens: the upper part of most of these frames is sky or bright surface water, so the clock stays readable, while the deep blues and storm greys run dark enough for night use. Turquoise pieces glow on bright screens; the sunset wave is the warm evening pick.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so foam, reef colour and wet-sand detail stay sharp on any modern phone. Set the beach you are counting down to, or the storm you would rather watch than sail — and swap whenever the tide of your mood turns.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the shoreline stays in frame.
