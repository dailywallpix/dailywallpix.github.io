About this collection

This collection brings together 62 fantasy wallpapers made for phone screens — worlds that don't exist, yet: floating islands drifting above the clouds, a crystal cave lit from within, an enchanted forest, a sky castle, and an underwater city under glass-blue water. The map keeps unfolding — a tree village in the canopy, a forest portal glowing between the trunks, a gate standing open to the moon, a dragon's perch, and fairy lights strung through the dark — every frame an invitation to somewhere else.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so castles, islands and portals rise the full height of the frame instead of being cropped into a postcard. Fantasy art suits lock screens that want a story: the scenes are deep and layered, foreground to far-off spires, with the sky — where the clock sits — usually the calmest part of the painting. The crystal cave and moon gate glow on OLED screens; the enchanted forest runs green and quiet.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so glow, mist and fine painted detail stay sharp on any modern phone. Choose your world — the island, the castle, the gate — and step through it fifty times a day.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
