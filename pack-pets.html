About this collection

This collection brings together 62 pet wallpapers made for phone screens — instant mood repair: a golden puppy sitting in the flowers, a kitten with enormous eyes, a French bulldog tilting its head, and a husky puppy standing in fresh snow. The whole household is here — a corgi mid-smile, a kitten tangled in yarn, a bunny in the grass, a parrot in full colour, a goldfish in its bowl, and a hamster with its cheeks full — every one a close portrait, because with pets the face is the whole point.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so your favourite animal fills the frame instead of being cropped to an ear. Close portraits sit naturally below the clock, and the bright garden and flower scenes keep the top of the screen light and readable. These are the wallpapers people actually keep: the puppy pieces are the cheerful daytime picks, the snow husky runs cooler and calmer, and the kitten macro is pure soft focus.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so fur, whiskers and wet noses stay sharp on any modern phone. Pick the pet you have, the pet you want, or the pet you miss — and rotate the whole menagerie through the month.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so your pet's face stays in frame.
