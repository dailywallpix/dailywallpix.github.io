About this collection

This collection brings together 62 car wallpapers made for phone screens — a neon sports car under city lights, a classic muscle car at golden hour, a supercar in the rain, a vintage pickup by the barn, a lowrider at dusk, a drift car trailing smoke, a rally car in the snow, and a camper van parked by the lake. From golden-era limos and foggy oldtimers to electric concepts, monster trucks, and open-wheel racers, every car here is shot like it is the hero of its own movie.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the car's lines and headlights fill the frame instead of getting cropped away. The collection suits every kind of car lover: neon night scenes and highway light streaks for the city driver, desert highways, canyon runs, and mountain passes for the road-trip dreamer, and garage light, engine bays, and restorations for anyone who loves the machine itself. Dark scenes look especially good on OLED screens and dark-mode home screens.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so paint, chrome, and rain-lit reflections stay sharp on any modern phone. Pick the car you would drive if the garage had no limits — and change it whenever a new dream car comes along.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
