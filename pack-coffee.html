About this collection

This collection brings together 62 coffee wallpapers made for phone screens — warm drinks and soft light: coffee by a rainy window, latte art poured in a rosetta, a slow breakfast tray, books beside a full cup, and dark beans in the roaster. The rituals are all here — an espresso pour in mid-stream, cappuccino foam, tea time for the other camp, a golden bakery case, and the coziest desk you have ever seen, cup steaming beside the keyboard.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the cup and its setting fill the frame instead of being cropped away. Coffee scenes run warm and mid-toned, which flatters a lock screen at any hour: bright enough to feel like morning, soft enough not to glare at night, with the window and café pieces leaving gentle light at the top where the clock sits. The latte-art macros are the design picks; the rainy window is the mood one.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so foam detail, steam and bakery gold stay sharp on any modern phone. Set the café you wish you were sitting in — first coffee of the day, every time you pick up the phone.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the cup stays in frame.
