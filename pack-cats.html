About this collection

This collection brings together 52 cute cat wallpapers made for phone screens — a tabby in golden window light, a Maine Coon's magnificent mane, a calico wandering the garden, wide blue eyes, a sunset silhouette on a rooftop, kittens tangled in yarn, a sleepy cat peeking out of a blanket, and a kitten watching the world from a windowsill. From pedigree faces to scruffy little rescues, every cat here looks like it owns the room it is sitting in.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the cat's face stays in frame instead of getting cropped away. The collection suits every mood: a calm, sleepy cat for a quiet home screen, a playful kitten for a phone that should make you smile every time you unlock it, and golden-hour cats for anyone who loves warm light. They work just as well as a gift of a sort — set one on a cat lover's phone and watch their face.

All 52 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so whiskers and fur stay sharp on any modern phone. Pick the cat that looks most like yours — or the one you wish was yours — and change it whenever a new favourite comes along.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
