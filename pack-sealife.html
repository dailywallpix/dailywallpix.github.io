About this collection

This collection brings together 62 sea life wallpapers made for phone screens — the other seventy percent of the planet: a turtle cruising over the reef, jellyfish glowing in dark water, a whale crossing the light rays, and a coral reef in full colour. The cast keeps swimming — dolphins at sunset, a golden seahorse in the grass, an octopus uncurled, a shark's clean silhouette, clownfish in their anemone, and a stingray gliding over sand — the whole reef, framed for the screen you carry everywhere.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so animals hang in open water the way they should, with deep blue above and below. Underwater scenes flatter lock screens: the water column is naturally darkest at the top, where the clock stays crisp, and the glowing pieces — jellyfish, sunlit whale — were made for OLED screens. The reef scenes are the colour picks; the shark and stingray run minimal and calm.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so scale, fin and coral detail stay sharp on any modern phone. Pick your creature — the gentle turtle, the alien jellyfish, the apex shark — and let the reef move in.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the animal stays in frame.
