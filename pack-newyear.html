About this collection

This collection holds 254 Happy New Year 2027 wallpapers for the biggest wallpaper week of the year — fireworks over cities, beaches, lakes, and bridges, black-and-gold glitter, champagne toasts at midnight, the ball drop, sparklers in hand, wish lanterns rising, and the quieter side of the turn of the year: a first sunrise hike, an empty page at dawn, and a fresh road into the morning.

Every wallpaper is made portrait for phone screens (1152 × 2048), so fireworks burst above the clock instead of being cut off, and gold confetti fills the frame rather than the edges. The loud, glittering scenes suit countdown week itself — the party, the toast, the last firework — while the sunrise and fresh-start scenes carry well into January, when the year is new and the phone should feel like it too.

All 254 are free in full quality with no watermark. Pick a fireworks scene for New Year's Eve, switch to a sunrise for January 1, and keep the gold-and-black look for as long as the new-year feeling lasts.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
