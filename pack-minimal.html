About this collection

This collection brings together 62 minimal wallpapers made for phone screens — for screens that breathe: flowing gradient waves, soft pastel shapes, a line-art sun, a beige arch, silky colour mesh, gold moon phases, soft 3D ribbons, and one perfect dune. Nothing here shouts — a terracotta arch against a plain wall, a sage circle, a single curve of sand — each piece is one idea, calmly stated, with most of the frame left empty on purpose.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, and minimal art is the natural partner for both: all that quiet space means the clock, widgets and app icons sit on a clean field with nothing fighting behind them. The pastel and beige pieces keep a home screen light and ordered; the moon phases and darker gradients do the same job at night. If a busy photo wallpaper has ever annoyed you, this is the antidote.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so gradients stay perfectly smooth — no banding — and line work stays hairline-sharp on any modern phone. Start with the gradient waves if you want colour without content, or the dune if you want almost nothing at all, beautifully.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the shape stays where you want it.
