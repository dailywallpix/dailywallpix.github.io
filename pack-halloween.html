About this collection

This collection holds 52 Halloween wallpapers made for phone screens — haunted houses under a giant moon, pumpkin-lit forest paths, a gothic castle above the sea at dusk, black cats on moonlit fences, witches' cottages, graveyards in soft fog, candy-covered porches, and the cozy cottage everyone secretly wants to live in. The mix runs from properly spooky to warmly autumnal, so there is a scare level for every phone: ghosts, skeletons, and haunted libraries for the horror fans, and pumpkins, lantern light, and harvest moons for everyone else.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so the haunted house, the pumpkin path, or the black cat stays in frame instead of getting cropped away. The darker scenes suit the season especially well — deep night skies, glowing windows, and jack-o'-lantern light look striking on a phone at night, and they sit beautifully on dark-mode home screens. Pick a spooky one for October itself, or a cozy cottage scene for the whole autumn.

All 52 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark. Save your favourite now and it will still be waiting when October comes round again — a haunted house for the big night, a pumpkin path for the walk home, or a black cat for a little everyday magic.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the main scene stays in frame.
