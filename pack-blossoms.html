About this collection

This collection brings together 62 blossom wallpapers made for phone screens — spring at full power: a cherry blossom street under a pink canopy, magnolias opening, a wisteria tunnel in violet, and blossoms hanging over a still lake. The pack follows the season flower by flower — a dewy tulip up close, a train passing under the sakura, petal rain falling, white almond bloom, a plum branch, jacaranda in purple, a peach orchard in rows, hydrangeas, and a lavender field running to the horizon.

Every wallpaper is portrait-shaped for a phone lock screen or home screen, so branches and canopies arch over the frame the way they do on a real spring walk — and handily leave the top of the screen soft and light, where the clock sits easy to read. The pink sakura scenes are the gentle everyday picks; the wisteria and jacaranda pieces run richer and deeper for anyone who wants colour with weight; the tulip macro and almond bloom keep things minimal and bright.

All 62 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so petal detail and soft spring light stay crisp on any modern phone. Blossom season lasts a few weeks outside — on your lock screen it can stay as long as you like. Start with the cherry street, and rotate through the season as each flower's week arrives.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the blossom canopy stays in frame.
