About this collection

This collection brings together 24 cute wallpapers made for phone screens — a kitten wearing a pink bow, a puppy asleep in a basket of flowers, a bunny next to a pastel cupcake, a baby panda hugging bamboo, and a tiny hedgehog holding a flower. Around them sit the kawaii pieces — a chibi cat, a smiling strawberry, a friendly little ghost — and the pastel patterns: hearts, bows, rainbows, stars and moons.

Cute wallpaper for girls is its own style, and this pack is built exactly for it: soft pinks and lavenders, rounded shapes, blush cheeks and sleepy baby animals — nothing sharp, nothing loud. The pastel pieces sit calmly behind icons and widgets, and the animal portraits are framed to sit below the clock, so the lock screen stays as sweet as the wallpaper.

All 24 are free in full quality (1152 × 2048) with no watermark, so fur, petals and pastel gradients stay soft and crisp on any modern phone. Start with the kitten in the bow, trade to the bunny for spring, or keep the pastel hearts all year — and swap whenever the mood changes.

How to set one as your wallpaper

  1. Tap Download on the wallpaper you like — it saves in full quality.
  2. Open it in your Photos or Gallery app and tap Use as wallpaper (or Set as wallpaper).
  3. Choose Lock screen, Home screen, or both, then adjust the crop so the cute subject sits below the clock.
